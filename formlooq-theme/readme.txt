A lightweight, standalone bilingual product and documentation theme for formlooq.ir.

== Installation ==

1. Upload formlooq-theme.zip through Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme.
2. Activate Form LOOQ Theme.
3. Open Settings > Permalinks and save once if the /en/ and /fa/ routes do not appear immediately.

The theme does not require Elementor, Polylang, or any other plugin. English and Persian routes, content, logos, direction, navigation, and metadata are included.
